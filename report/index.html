How reliable are the answers?

Neutral share: valence answers on the middle option. The middle letter is the same in the original and reversed option order, so a middle-letter bias can't be separated from real indifference here. Order gap: mean |original − reversed| item score. Persistence flip: share of persistence items whose most common answer changes when option order is reversed.
